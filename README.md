**Sunset Bay** — an original sun-soaked open-world crime game (3D, three.js, fully offline). You are a newcomer in the coastal city of Sunset Bay, running jobs for the harbour fixer Marisol: pick up a package at the pier and deliver it across town, steal a red sports car for the chop shop, then survive a 3-star police chase. The city has a road grid with traffic and pedestrians, a beach and pier, a central plaza, and the ocean to the south. Steal cars, punch, crash, and the wanted stars climb — police cruisers with flashing light bars hunt you down, and staying clean lets the heat fade. Progress (money + missions) saves to localStorage.

Controls: **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** camera (pointer lock) · **Shift** run · **E** enter / exit car · **F / Click** punch · **Space** brake · **Esc** pause. Sound is synthesized with WebAudio (engine hum, sirens, impacts) — no audio files.
