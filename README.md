**Sunset Bay** — an original sun-soaked open-world crime game (3D, three.js, fully offline). You are a newcomer in the coastal city of Sunset Bay, running jobs for the harbour fixer Marisol: pick up a package at the pier and deliver it across town, steal a red sports car for the chop shop, survive a 3-star police chase — and finish with the new fourth job, the **Sunset Sprint**: race three checkpoints down Ocean Drive to the pier in 90 seconds at 2 wanted stars, for $1,500. The city has a road grid with traffic and pedestrians, a beach and pier, a central plaza, and the ocean to the south. Steal cars, punch, crash, and the wanted stars climb — police cruisers with flashing light bars hunt you down, and staying clean lets the heat fade. Progress (money + missions) saves to localStorage.

**Golden-hour look:** a shader sunset sky with a low sun glowing over the sea, warm haze and long shadows; taller downtown towers with lit windows, rooftop AC units, water towers and antennas, neon signs for the town's own places (SUNSET BAY, OCEAN HOTEL, MARISOL'S, PIER 9, TACO BAY, NEON MOTEL, BAY CINEMA, SUNSET GARAGE), the Ocean Hotel with its pool deck, a shack at the end of the pier, beach umbrellas, and sunlight glittering on the water. Cars are more detailed (slanted windscreens, glowing headlights, red tail lights, mirrors, twin exhausts, spoilers, hubcaps, plates), cruisers carry a bigger light bar with alternating red/blue halo glow and SBPD door decals, and the action leaves exhaust smoke, tyre skid marks, crash sparks, small fireballs and gold fireworks when a job is done. The HUD shows a clear MISSION n/4 panel, throbbing wanted stars, and a minimap with coastline, sand, plaza and a pulsing mission marker.

Controls: **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** camera (pointer lock) · **Shift** run · **E** enter / exit car · **F / Click** punch · **Space** brake · **Esc** pause. Sound is synthesized with WebAudio (engine hum, sirens, impacts) — no audio files.
